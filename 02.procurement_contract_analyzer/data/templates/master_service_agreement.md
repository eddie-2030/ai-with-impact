# Master Service Agreement (Standard)

## Definitions
Key terms used in this Agreement shall have the meanings set forth below.

## Scope of Services
Supplier shall provide the Services as described in the applicable Order Forms.

## Service Levels
Supplier shall meet or exceed the Service Levels specified, including availability and response times.

## Fees and Payment
Fees shall be invoiced monthly in arrears. Payments due within thirty (30) days of invoice date.

## Confidentiality
Each party agrees to keep Confidential Information secret and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

## Data Protection
Supplier shall process Customer Personal Data solely on documented instructions and comply with the DPA.

## Security
Supplier shall implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards.

## Intellectual Property
Except as expressly provided, each party retains ownership of its Background IP. Deliverables are assigned to Customer.

## Indemnification
Supplier shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Customer from third-party claims arising from the Services.

## Limitation of Liability
Except for uncapped categories (IP infringement, breach of confidentiality, data protection), liability is capped at 12 months of fees.

## Term and Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach uncured within thirty (30) days; Customer may terminate for convenience with notice.

## Governing Law
Governing law: New York; venue: New York County courts.

## Miscellaneous
Entire Agreement; amendments in writing; order of precedence.
